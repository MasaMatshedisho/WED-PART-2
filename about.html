About Glow & Go Beauty Studio

Dedicated to helping our clients look and feel their best through professional and affordable self-care treatments.

Our Story

Founded in Pretoria in 2023, Glow & Go Beauty Studio was established by a qualified beauty therapist to deliver personal care services in a warm, welcoming environment.

We aim to take the stress out of routine beauty care by providing efficient, high-quality manicures, facials, and makeup services tailored to your busy lifestyle.

Our Mission & Values

Mission: To provide accessible, high-standard beauty care where every client feels confident and valued.

  • Quality & Safety: Continuous professional development and medical-grade equipment hygiene.
  • Ethical Products: Cruelty-free serums and organic skin preparations.
  • Client Satisfaction: Tailored treatments designed around individual skin types and personal preferences.
